Simulation Guidelines

1. Select Train point or Test point above and click on the canvas to add data points by yourself.
2. Select Hide point and click existing points to hide them from existence. You can double tap on the hidden point space to add it again and make it unhidden.
3. Click your currently selected point mode again to unselect it. You can now click and drag to pan the canvas freely.
4. Use the regression buttons below to fit models to your points.
5. Toggle the Weight and Bias scroll bars to manually adjust the line and understand how the regression plane responds.
